I'm Luigi (werew), a security engineer based in London.

This blog is a place for me to document research, writeups, and thoughts on
security, computer science, or anything else I find worth writing down.

## About me

I've spent the last several years at Meta working on product, platform, and AI
security: threat modeling and reviewing generative AI services, driving security
roadmaps for [Hermes](https://engineering.fb.com/2022/07/20/security/hermes-quicksort-to-run-doom/)
(the JavaScript engine behind React Native), building security telemetry for
mobile apps, rolling out
[secret scanning](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2021/11/09/meta-joins-github-secret-scanning-program-as-partner/)
across CI/CD, and hunting for vulnerabilities, including publicly disclosed
CVEs in [WhatsApp](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2019-3568) and
[Folly](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2021-24036). More recently a lot of
my time goes to agentic systems and the security of LLM
integrations.

Before that I interned at Quarkslab working on
[reverse engineering tooling](https://blog.quarkslab.com/exploring-execution-trace-analysis.html)
and [research on automatic deobfuscation](https://archive.bar/pdfs/bar2020-preprint9.pdf).
I hold an MSc in Security & Privacy (EIT / Twente / Trento) and a BSc in
Computer Science from the University of Strasbourg. I've also played my share
of CTFs.

## On the use of AI

I employ AI in my day-to-day activities, including the creation of content for
this blog. In practice I use AI to correct drafts, polish and enhance content,
and automate experiments: running variations, processing results, building
tooling I'd otherwise skip.

Ideas, research questions, hypotheses, conclusions, and opinions are mine.
Everything published here is reviewed and adjusted by me before it goes out, so
blame me for the mistakes.
